# IEEE INSAT SB equipment reservations

## Product

A responsive equipment desk for IEEE INSAT Student Branch members and Board operators. Members check availability for a chosen pickup and return window, request equipment for themselves or an active chapter, and track the request. Board operators approve quantities and identify physical assets at pickup, manage chapters and inventory, record collection and return scans, and review the schedule and audit trail.

## Visual direction

- Use a light interface with IEEE blue and navy, light neutral surfaces, cyan highlights, and INSAT violet for borrowed materials.
- Use compact dashboard headings, factual page descriptions, uppercase section labels, and clear readable body text.
- Present catalogue equipment as compact horizontal rows; keep time-window controls, reservation rows, and inventory labels dense and scannable.
- Use the system sans-serif stack throughout the interface.
- Use consistent rounded cards, restrained borders, and subtle shadows. Use IEEE blue for primary actions.

## Interaction rules

- Show availability for the complete selected time window and display all times in Africa/Tunis.
- Approve quantities first. Identify physical materials at pickup using scanned QR labels or the PC handover dialog.
- Use semantic links, buttons, labelled fields, visible focus, clear disabled states, and accessible dialog names.
- Keep member and Board navigation usable on narrow screens without hiding destinations.
- Refer to the member's pending choices as an item selection, never a basket or shopping cart.
- Use operational product copy; avoid slogans, promotional phrasing, and synthetic-sounding language.
- Include loading, empty, and error feedback for data-backed views.

## Product boundaries

- This application has its own identity, database, users, and reservation workflow.
- Do not reintroduce RAS Logistics modules, branding, data, or deployment assumptions.
- Do not imply that email sign-in works until Brevo delivery is configured.
